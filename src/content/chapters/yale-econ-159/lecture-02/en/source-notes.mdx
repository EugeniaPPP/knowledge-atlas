The main source is **Ben Polak, Yale ECON 159, Lecture 2—Putting Yourselves into Other People’s Shoes**, September 10, 2007. [Video and transcript](https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159/lecture-2). The supplied **blackboard notes 02** (pp. 1–2) were also checked. Section timestamps refer to the original lecture, not to reading time.

Editorial clarifications distinguish “dominates another strategy” from “is dominant against all alternatives,” and explain why weak dominance and rationality do not automatically imply a unique choice in a real group. The interface compares **pure strategies**; dominance by mixed strategies is outside this chapter’s scope.
