## Sources and editorial notes

This chapter is based on **Ben Polak, *Game Theory*, Yale ECON 159, Lecture 1 (2007)**. Sources include the transcript, *Summary & Lessons of the First Class* (3 pages), and *Blackboard Notes — Lecture 1* (2 pages). The matrices and numerical payoffs are retained from the original materials; the explanations and transitions form a standalone textbook narrative rather than a retelling of the class.
