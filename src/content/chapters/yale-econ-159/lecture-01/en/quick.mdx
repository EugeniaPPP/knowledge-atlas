<section className="quick-section">
## The central model

<div className="quick-matrix"><PayoffMatrix model={models['grade-game']} /></div>

**α strictly dominates β:** against the partner’s α, 0 &gt; −1; against their β, 3 &gt; 1. Each chooses α → (0,0), although mutual β would give (1,1).

This is a prisoner’s dilemma; (0,0) is Pareto inefficient.
</section>

<section className="quick-section">
## Four principles

> **01. Do not play a strictly dominated strategy.**
>
> Another strategy gives you a higher payoff whatever your partner chooses.

> **02. Rational individual choices can lead to a bad collective outcome.**
>
> Each pursues their own interests, yet both could be better off.

> **03. First establish what “winning” means to each player.**
>
> Players can value the same outcomes differently.

> **04. Put yourself in the other player’s shoes.**
>
> Analyze their payoffs and predict their choice.
</section>
